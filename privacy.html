Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

What Stubs reads

When you connect a Gmail account, Stubs asks Google for read-only access to your email (the gmail.readonly permission) and your email address. Stubs uses that access only to search for ticket emails from Tix (tix.com) and read those messages. It does not read, store, or look at any other email.

What Stubs keeps, and where

From each Tix email, Stubs keeps the show name, date, venue, order number, seat details, and the ticket barcode. It stores these, along with your Google sign-in token, only on your iPhone (the token is kept in the iOS Keychain). Stubs has no server, and the developer never receives your email, your tickets, or your Google token.

Who Stubs talks to

  • Google, to sign you in and to search and read your Tix emails.
  • Tix, to load the e-ticket page linked in your own order emails, so Stubs can show each seat's QR code.

Stubs does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.

Google API data

Stubs' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show your tickets in the app, is never used for advertising, and is never read by a person.

Removing your data

Disconnecting an account in Stubs (Accounts → swipe to remove) deletes its token and asks Google to revoke Stubs' access. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can also revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
